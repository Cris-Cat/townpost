Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CityBoard ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our community noticeboard platform.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an event or notice to CityBoard, we collect:

  • Event Information: Title, description, category, date/time, and location details you provide
  • Images: Photos you upload to illustrate your event
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and device information (for security and bot prevention)
  • CAPTCHA Data: Proof-of-work solutions to verify you are human (not stored permanently)

We do NOT collect: Personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily include it in your event description.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Display your event on our public noticeboard
  • Moderate content to ensure it meets our community guidelines
  • Prevent spam and bot submissions
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our platform

4. Data Storage and Security

Your event data is stored on our secure servers. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction of data. Images are automatically stripped of EXIF metadata (including GPS coordinates) upon upload to protect your privacy.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Edit your event using the secret edit link provided after submission
  • Request deletion of your event by contacting us
  • Know what data we hold about your submissions

6. Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Our CAPTCHA system (Altcha) is self-hosted and privacy-friendly, using proof-of-work verification without external data sharing.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@cityboard.example
Address: [Your Address Here]

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
